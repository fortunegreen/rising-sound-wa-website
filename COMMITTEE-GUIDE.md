# Updating the Rising Sound WA website

You can add and change shows, artists and sessions yourself. No coding.

## Signing in
1. Go to **https://admin.risingsoundwa.com.au**
2. Click **Sign in with GitHub** and use your GitHub account. If you don't have one, make a free account at
   github.com and send Penny your username so she can give you access.
3. The first time, GitHub asks you to approve access. Click Authorise.

## Add a new show
1. Click **Events** → **Create**.
2. Fill in: Title, Date, Time (as people should read it, e.g. "7:00pm"), Venue, a short description, Price
   (e.g. "Free" or "$25"), Region, and tick **Free entry** if it is free.
3. Paste the ticket link if there is one. If you leave it empty, the show gets an "Add to calendar" button instead.
4. Add the poster image if you have one. **Always fill in the picture description (alt text).**
5. Optional: start time, end time and performers help Google show the event in search.
6. Click **Create** (or **Save** when editing).

The site updates by itself within a minute or two. Refresh the public site to check.

## After a show has happened
Open the event and add photos (with a short description and a photographer credit) and video links. It moves
to **Past events** on its own, with a "Photos & video" button.

## Change the Home or About page wording
- **Home page**: headline, intro, the two buttons (the words and where they go), the hero photo, the "Join the room" box, the Rising Sound Sessions intro, and the four "What we do" boxes.
- **About page**: heading, the paragraphs, the highlighted quote, the photo and caption, the values, and the committee intro.
- To link a button to another page of the site, type the address starting with a slash, e.g. `/whats-on/`. For another website, paste the full address.
- The Google search title and description for each page are not editable here. Ask Penny.

## Other things you can edit
- **Sessions**: artist, song, Vimeo or YouTube link, still image.
- **Artists**: photo, bio, genre, area, links. Email is in two parts (the bit before the @, and the bit after).
- **Settings**: the scrolling ticker, contact email, social links.
- **Membership**: prices and what each tier includes.

## Photo tips
- Use **JPEG** photos. Camera photos are fine. The site shrinks big ones for you.
- Don't upload PNGs bigger than about 1 MB.
- Write what's in the picture in the description box ("Band on the shop floor at Midland Records").

## If something goes wrong
- Edits go live straight away, so double-check dates and spelling before saving.
- If you break something, don't worry. Everything is saved in history and can be undone. Tell Penny what you changed.
- If you can't sign in, check Penny has given your GitHub account access.
